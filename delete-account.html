Delete your account

You can ask Ride Express to delete the account you use in the Ride Express passenger or driver app. This page explains how to send that request, what you should include, and what happens to your information.

Request account deletion

To request deletion, email Ride Express at admin.contact@ridexpress.co.uk.

Use the button below to open your email app with the message addressed to Ride Express. Send the message from the email address registered on your account whenever you can, so we can match the request to the right account.

Request Account Deletion

The message is addressed to admin.contact@ridexpress.co.uk with the subject “Ride Express Account Deletion Request”.

What to include

So that Ride Express can find the correct account, include all of the following in your email:

  • Registered email address
  • Registered phone number
  • Full name
  • A clear statement that you are requesting account deletion

Account verification

Ride Express may verify account ownership before processing the request. We do this so that an account is not deleted because of a request from someone else. We may ask you to confirm details that match the account, or to reply from the registered email address, before the deletion goes ahead.

What is deleted or anonymised

When a verified deletion request is completed, Ride Express generally deletes or anonymises:

  • User profile information
  • Contact information
  • Account authentication information
  • Saved account information
  • Personal location information where it is no longer required

What may be retained

Certain information may be retained when required or permitted by law, or when it is necessary for legitimate purposes, such as:

  • Financial and transaction records
  • Fraud prevention
  • Dispute resolution
  • Legal or regulatory obligations
  • Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims

How long retained information is kept

Information that is retained is limited to what is needed for the applicable purpose. Ride Express deletes or anonymises that information when the retention requirement ends.

Removing specific information

If you want specific data removed without deleting your entire account, contact Ride Express at admin.contact@ridexpress.co.uk and describe your request. Tell us what you want removed and the email address or phone number on the account. We will let you know what can be removed and what, if anything, must be kept for the reasons described above.
